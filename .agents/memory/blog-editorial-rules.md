---
name: Blog editorial rules
description: Preserve article copy during UI work and verify permitted stock-photo sources.
---

Keep existing article bodies unchanged during blog UI upgrades. Add summaries and image details in frontmatter rather than rewriting the articles.

**Why:** The user explicitly separated the blog presentation upgrade from editorial changes.

**How to apply:** Compare the article bodies before and after UI changes; source takeaways from the existing article rather than introducing new claims.

Blog cover photographs must be free images from Unsplash or Pexels, with accurate photographer attribution.

**Why:** The user restricted cover sources and requested real, licensed photographs with credits. Image-search descriptions can misidentify the photographer.

**How to apply:** Verify the original photo page and free license before downloading; use that page's photographer name, not an unverified search-result attribution.
