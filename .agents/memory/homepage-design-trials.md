name: Homepage design trials
description: Approval boundary for the problem-section redesign.
---

Try the problem-section redesign on the main homepage only. Do not replicate it on other market pages until the user finalizes it.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a main-page trial before approval and replication.

**How to apply:** Keep market pages unchanged while iterating on this design; wait for approval before extending it.

Prioritize a prominent, readable comparison over forcing the entire section into one laptop viewport.

**Why:** The viewport-height-based sizing made the comparison look like a thumbnail on the user's laptop, and they rejected the result.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce height-dependent width shrinking to satisfy the earlier one-screen goal. Preserve the desktop comparison's intended width even when the section needs scrolling.
