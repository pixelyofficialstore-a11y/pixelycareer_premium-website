Try the problem-section redesign on the main homepage only. Do not replicate it on other market pages until the user finalizes it.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a main-page trial before approval and replication.

**How to apply:** Keep market pages unchanged while iterating on this design; wait for approval before extending it.

For the split laptop layout, fit the full A4 comparison and text into the viewport below the header. Keep the headline at the hero's desktop size and allow wrapping.

**Why:** The user superseded the earlier wide comparison trial with an explicit full A4, one-screen split layout. When asked about the font-size conflict, they chose “Hero size, allow wrapping.”

**How to apply:** Size the portrait CV from available height, not a landscape thumbnail. Do not shrink the headline merely to force two lines. Keep the main-page-only approval boundary.
