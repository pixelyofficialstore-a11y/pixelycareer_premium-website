Recruiters spend seconds on a first read. These ten mistakes are the fastest way to lose those seconds.

## 1. A two column layout

Looks modern, but ATS systems often read it in the wrong order.
**Fix:** use a single column.

## 2. A generic objective

"Seeking a challenging role in a reputed company" tells the recruiter nothing.
**Fix:** write a 3 line summary with your title, experience and one result.

## 3. Duties instead of results

<BeforeAfter
  before="Responsible for managing customer accounts."
  after="Managed 60 B2B accounts and grew renewals by 22% in one year."
/>

**Fix:** start each bullet with a verb and end with a result.

## 4. No keywords from the job ad

**Fix:** match the skills and tools the ad asks for, honestly.

## 5. Skill bars and ratings

"Excel: 4 out of 5 stars" means nothing to a recruiter or software.
**Fix:** list skills as plain text.

## 6. Too much personal information

Religion, marital status and passport numbers are rarely needed.
**Fix:** keep only what employers use for that country.

## 7. Inconsistent dates and formatting

**Fix:** use one date format and one font throughout.

## 8. An unprofessional email

**Fix:** use firstname.lastname@email.com.

## 9. Typos

**Fix:** read it out loud, then ask someone else to check.

## 10. One resume for every job

**Fix:** tailor your summary and skills for each application. It takes ten minutes and makes a real difference.

<Callout type="tip" title="Quick self check">
Could a stranger understand your job, your level and your best result within 10 seconds? If not, rewrite the top third.
</Callout>

## The bottom line

None of these mistakes is hard to fix. Fix the format first, then the content. A clean layout with clear results puts you ahead of most applicants.
