Your About section is where a recruiter decides whether to message you. Most people either leave it empty or fill it with buzzwords. Here is a simple formula and five examples.

## The 3 part formula

1. **Who you are:** your role and experience in one sentence
2. **Proof:** two or three specific results
3. **What's next:** the roles or projects you want

<Callout type="tip" title="Avoid these words">
Passionate, hardworking, results driven, team player. Show it with results instead.
</Callout>

## Example 1: Graduate

> I'm a recent Business Administration graduate focused on marketing and data. During my internship at a retail brand, I built weekly sales dashboards and helped plan a campaign that grew newsletter signups by 30%.
>
> I'm now looking for a junior marketing or marketing analyst role where I can keep learning and work with data every day.

## Example 2: Mid career professional

> I'm a Financial Analyst with 6 years of experience in budgeting, forecasting and management reporting.
>
> I've cut month end close from 10 to 6 days, identified $1.2M in cost savings and built Power BI reports leadership uses every week.
>
> I'm open to senior FP&A and finance manager roles in Chicago or remote.

## Example 3: Career changer

> After 8 years as a teacher, I'm moving into learning and development. Teaching taught me to design training, present to large groups and measure progress.
>
> I recently completed a Certificate in Instructional Design and built three online courses for a local nonprofit.
>
> I'm looking for L&D or training coordinator roles.

## Example 4: Senior leader

> I lead operations teams that turn complex processes into simple, measurable systems. Over 15 years, I've managed teams of up to 200 people across logistics and manufacturing.
>
> Recent results include a 35% productivity increase and $750K in annual cost savings through automation and vendor redesign.
>
> I'm interested in COO and VP Operations roles.

## Example 5: Gulf job seeker

> I'm a Civil Engineer with 7 years of experience on residential and commercial projects across Riyadh and Dubai. I've supervised sites of 80+ workers and delivered projects worth up to SAR 40M on schedule.
>
> I hold an active Saudi Council of Engineers registration and I'm open to project engineer roles across the GCC.

## Before you publish

- Read it out loud. If it sounds stiff, simplify.
- Check that your top skills appear at least once.
- Make sure it matches your CV.

## The bottom line

A great About section is short, specific and human. Use the formula, borrow the structure of the example closest to you, and replace every claim with something you can prove.
