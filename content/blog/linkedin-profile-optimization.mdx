Recruiters search LinkedIn every day. An optimized profile shows up in those searches and gives them a reason to reach out. Work through these steps in order.

## 1. Photo and banner

- A clear, recent head and shoulders photo with a plain background
- A simple banner related to your field or your city skyline

## 2. Headline

Your headline is the most searched field on your profile. Use your target title and two or three core skills.

## 3. Location and job preferences

Set the city where you want to work. Recruiters filter by location. Then add your target job titles under Open to Work, and choose **recruiters only** if you are employed.

## 4. About section

Write it in the first person, in three short parts:

1. What you do and for how long
2. Two or three results you are proud of
3. What you are looking for next

Keep it under 250 words, with keywords used naturally.

## 5. Experience

Copy your strongest CV bullets, not your job description. Add a one line summary of each company if it is not well known.

<Callout type="tip" title="Match your CV">
Same titles, same dates, same story. Recruiters compare both.
</Callout>

## 6. Skills

Add up to 50, but pin your top three, matching your headline. Ask colleagues to endorse those three first.

## 7. Recommendations

Two or three short recommendations from managers or clients add real proof. Offer to write one in return.

## 8. Custom URL

Edit your public profile URL to linkedin.com/in/firstnamelastname and add it to your CV.

## Quick checklist

- Headline with target title and skills
- Location set to your target city
- About section with results
- Experience matching your CV
- Top 3 skills pinned
- Open to Work, recruiters only

## The bottom line

An optimized profile works for you while you sleep. Start with the headline and About section today. Those two changes alone usually increase profile views within weeks.
