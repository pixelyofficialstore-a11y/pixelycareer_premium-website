Your headline appears next to your name almost everywhere on LinkedIn: in search results, comments, connection requests and messages. Recruiters often search by job title and skills, so the words in your headline matter.

## The formula

**Target role | Specialism or industry | Key skills or proof**

Keep it clear before it's clever. A recruiter should understand what you do in two seconds.

## Examples by role

- **Accountant:** Accountant | Financial Reporting & Reconciliation | ACCA Part-Qualified
- **Civil engineer:** Civil Engineer | Residential & Commercial Construction | Site Supervision & QS
- **Customer support:** Customer Support Specialist | CRM & Complaint Resolution | Call Centre Operations
- **Teacher:** Mathematics Teacher | Secondary & O/A Level | Curriculum Planning
- **Software developer:** Full-Stack Developer | React & Node.js | Building Fast, Reliable Web Apps
- **HR:** HR Generalist | Recruitment & Onboarding | Employee Relations

## Examples by career stage

- **Fresh graduate:** BBA Graduate | Marketing & Data Analysis | Seeking Entry-Level Marketing Roles
- **Career changer:** Operations Professional Moving into Project Management | PMP Candidate | Process Improvement
- **Senior leader:** Head of Operations | Logistics & Supply Chain | Building High-Performing Teams Across the GCC

## What to avoid

- **Only buzzwords:** "Passionate visionary leader" tells a recruiter nothing they can search for.
- **Only your employer:** "Working at Company X" hides what you actually do.
- **Emoji walls:** one may be fine for creative roles; a row of them looks unprofessional elsewhere.

<Callout type="tip" title="Match your CV">
Use the same target job title in your LinkedIn headline and at the top of your CV. When a recruiter checks both, the story should match.
</Callout>

## Beyond the headline

The headline gets you found. What makes a recruiter message you is the rest of the profile: a clear About section, experience written as achievements, a professional photo, and the right skills listed.

## The bottom line

Lead with the job title you want, add your specialism, and finish with skills or proof. Then make sure the rest of your profile backs it up.
