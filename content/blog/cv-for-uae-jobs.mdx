The UAE hiring market is international and competitive. Recruiters in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah often receive applications from many countries for the same role, so a clear, well-targeted CV makes a real difference.

The points below describe **common practice**. Always follow the job posting.

## Format

- **Single column**, clean headings, real text. Many UAE employers and job boards use applicant tracking systems.
- **Two pages** is typical for most professionals.
- **Clear dates** (month and year) for every role.

## Personal details

UAE CVs often include more personal information than US or UK CVs:

- Full name, phone (with country code), email and current location
- **Nationality** and **visa status** (for example, "Employment visa, transferable" or "Visit visa")
- **Availability** or notice period
- A **professional photo**, if you choose to include one

<Callout type="note" title="Why visa status matters">
Employers often check whether you're already in the UAE and how quickly you can join. Stating it clearly saves them a question.
</Callout>

## Content

- **A targeted summary** naming the role you want and your years of experience.
- **Regional experience first**, if you have it: GCC roles, clients or projects.
- **Achievement-led bullet points** with scale and results.
- **Keywords** copied from UAE job postings for your role: job titles, tools, certifications.

## Job profiles

Many UAE recruiters search **LinkedIn**, **Indeed** and **Naukrigulf**. Keep your profiles consistent with your CV: same job titles, same dates, same story.

## Checklist before you apply

1. Target job title at the top of your CV
2. Phone with +971 or your country code, plus a professional email
3. Nationality, visa status and availability stated clearly
4. Single-column layout that passes the copy-paste test
5. Keywords from the posting, used naturally
6. LinkedIn, Indeed and Naukrigulf profiles updated to match

## The bottom line

A UAE CV is clear, practical and specific: who you are, where you are, when you can start, and what you've achieved.
